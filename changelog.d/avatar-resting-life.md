### Changed

- A generated avatar's resting face is now one of the friendly expressions: neutral, attentive, happy,
  excited, laughing, curious, proud or shy. Before, an agent could wear an angry, sad, scared, wary,
  confused, bored or sleepy face for good. Moods still change the face while they last.
- Resting avatars on screen now and then blink or make a small move (a hop, a sway, a stretch or a look
  around), one avatar at a time. Each agent always makes the same move. Nothing runs when the window is
  hidden or the system asks for reduced motion.
